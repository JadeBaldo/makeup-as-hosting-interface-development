ALTERED IMAGES — BLISTER STUDY PATCH 01

Apply ONLY to the separate altered-images-deformation-study-01 folder.
This patch does not update the webcam/performance project.

1. Close the study tab. Keep a copy of your existing study folder if you want
   to compare the old deformation.
2. Copy these FOUR files into altered-images-deformation-study-01 and choose
   Replace for matching names:
      index.html
      study.mjs
      surface-study.mjs
      surface-study.test.mjs
   The fourth is the updated optional geometry test, not needed to run the page.
3. Leave the existing vendor folder and serve.py in place.
4. Open index.html with Live Server. Refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R if the old study
   appears. The heading should say BLISTER PATHS 01 and the letter should be B.

DRAWING
Draw on the filled parts of the B with a mouse, touchscreen or stylus.
The path leaves a connected raised ridge. Hold or retrace to inflate it faster.
Previously drawn paths grow more slowly while you continue painting.
Crossing a hole or leaving the letter breaks contact; blank spaces stay empty.
Lift to freeze all attached growth. Detached fragments finish falling.
Clear & restart resets both the paths and falling material.
View angle is for inspection, and drawing remains mapped at different angles.

COLOUR
Choose cobalt/fuchsia, electric cyan/laser red, or ultraviolet/acid green.
The first colour is the text. The stroke begins darker and the growth brightens
within the same hue family. Text colour does not blend toward growth colour.

SCOPE
This is a procedural study: rupture is represented by a rapidly collapsing,
darker channel and released fragments. It does not yet cut physical holes or
simulate a tearing membrane. The path drives the letter's own surface; growth
is not scattered from randomly placed dot sites. Webcam mapping and distribution
across full titles come after visual review.

TESTING
Optional (Node.js): node surface-study.test.mjs
Checks the actual B geometry, connected paths, local growth, freeze-on-lift,
one-time rupture events, reset and finite geometry. Browser appearance and
real-time drawing responsiveness still need testing on your laptop.
